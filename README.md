### Hi, I'm Antonio Alcántara 👋

I'm a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Quantitative Methods at [CUNEF Universidad](https://www.cunef.edu/) in Madrid. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at DTU Wind and Energy Systems, working with Spyros Chatzivasileiadis on trustworthy machine learning for power systems, and I received my Ph.D. in Mathematical Engineering from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.

🔬 **Research:** machine learning for decision-making under uncertainty: uncertainty quantification and probabilistic forecasting, constraint learning and data-driven optimization, and surrogate models, mostly applied to energy and power systems.
